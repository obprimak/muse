# Content Map — Muse by Noran Shine
## What goes where + what to prepare

## HOME PAGE ( / )

### 1. Hero Section — FULL SCREEN VIDEO
**Placeholder:** Dark overlay + "Muse by Noran Shine" headline
**What to place:** 1 atmospheric video, 15–30 seconds, muted loop
**Recommendation:**
- Slow, cinematic movement — fabric flowing, soft light on skin, candle flicker, hands adjusting jewelry
- NOT a fast-paced highlight reel — slow and editorial is the mood
- Shoot in your studio, golden hour light or controlled warm lighting
- Encode as MP4 (H.264) + WebM (VP9), keep under 15MB
- Resolution: 1920×1080 minimum

### 2. Trust Signals Bar
**Placeholder:** 3 icons + short text
**What to place:** 3 trust phrases, no images needed
**Recommendation:**
  - "100% Private — Your images are never shared without your consent"
  - "All bodies welcome — Every woman is worthy of this experience"
  - "Tampa Bay's boudoir studio since [year]"

### 3. "What is Muse?" Intro Section
**Placeholder:** Headline + 2–3 sentences + 1 portrait image
**What to place:** 1 strong boudoir portrait (your best work, face optional)
**Recommendation:** Vertical crop, dark/moody tone. Subject looking confident, not exposed. This is the first impression of your work.

### 4. Style Quiz CTA Section
**Placeholder:** Headline + button
**What to place:** 1 mood image (collage or single)
**Recommendation:** 3–4 small images side by side showing different vibes (classic, bold, soft, editorial) — hint at the quiz options visually

### 5. "The Experience" Preview
**Placeholder:** 3 cards with icons
**What to place:** 3 short descriptions of the client journey + optional 3 photos
**Recommendation:**
  - Card 1: "We prepare together" — wardrobe consultation, studio prep
  - Card 2: "You shine in the studio" — the session itself
  - Card 3: "You receive art" — gallery delivery, products

### 6. Featured Gallery Strip
**Placeholder:** 5–6 image grid, dark background
**What to place:** 5–6 of your strongest boudoir images (varied styles)
**Recommendation:** Mix of close crops and wider shots. Show range — lace, fabric, minimal. No faces required.

### 7. Availability Counter
**Placeholder:** "X sessions available this month"
**What to place:** Number (managed manually or via Supabase)
**Recommendation:** Start with a real number you're comfortable with — creates urgency without false scarcity

### 8. Testimonials
**Placeholder:** 2–3 quote cards
**What to place:** Real client quotes (anonymized — first name or initials only)
**Recommendation:** Focus on how they FELT, not just "great photos." These are the most powerful testimonials for boudoir.

### 9. Final CTA
**Placeholder:** "Book Your Session" button
**What to place:** No image needed — dark section, gold text + button

## FAQ PAGE ( /faq )

**Placeholder:** Collapsible accordion, text only
**What to place:** No images needed
**Recommendation:** Include 15–20 questions. Key topics:
- What if I've never done this before?
- Do I need to be a certain size/shape?
- What do I wear? (reference the brand guide)
- Is it really private?
- What happens to my photos?
- Can I bring someone?
- How long is the session?
- When do I receive my images?
- What if I'm nervous?
- How do I prepare?
