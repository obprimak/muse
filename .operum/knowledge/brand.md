## Brand Voice

### Tone
Warm, intimate, empowering, and deeply personal. Never clinical, never transactional.
Speaks to women as a trusted friend — knowledgeable, reassuring, celebratory.

### Core Belief
Empowerment comes from how you FEEL, not how you look.
"It's not about the clothes. It's about how you feel."

### Key Brand Phrases (use in copy)
- "It's not about the clothes. It's about how you feel."
- "Empowerment isn't about how much skin you show — it's about how you feel in your own body."
- "Confidence is the sexiest thing a woman can wear."
- "You are ready to shine."
- "A sanctuary of self-discovery and expression where confidence and elegance take center stage."
- "An intimate, inspiring escape — a place to embrace your essence... celebrate your beauty... and create timeless portraits that are uniquely YOU."
- "This is your moment — and you deserve to shine in it."
- "We'll make sure you feel it too."

### Always
- Lead with emotion and safety, not product
- Celebrate every body type, every comfort level
- Make the woman feel seen and honored before the session begins
- Reference "your story" — sessions are personal narratives
- Use "we" — collaborative, not transactional

### Never
- Clinical language
- Pressure to show more skin
- Standard marketing/sales tone
- Generic beauty industry phrases

## Comfort Level Framework (from guide)
Three tiers, all equally valid:
1. **Mostly Dressed** — Cozy yet captivating. Silk slips, flowing robes, off-the-shoulder.
2. **Classic Boudoir** — Bra and panty sets, garter belts, lace.
3. **Bare Elegance** — Sheer bodysuits, soft fabric draping, implied nude. Artistry over exposure.
