# Privacy Policy — Muse by Noran Shine
## DRAFT — For founder review before publishing

Effective Date: [DATE]

Welcome to Muse by Noran Shine. Your privacy — and the privacy of your images — is something we take exceptionally seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information and photographs when you visit muse.noranshine.com or book a boudoir session with us.

Boudoir photography is intimate by nature. We recognize that the images created during your session are among the most personal photographs you will ever have taken. This policy reflects that — your images are yours, and we will never share them without your explicit written consent.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following:

**Personal Details:** Name, email address, phone number, and session preferences. Anonymous inquiries are welcome — you may contact us without providing your real name until you feel comfortable doing so.

**Booking & Session Information:** Session date, package selection, style preferences, and any details you choose to share about your vision for the session.

**Payment Information:** Processed securely through third-party payment providers (e.g., Stripe). We do not store your payment card details.

**Photography & Image Data:** All photographs taken during your boudoir session. These are treated as sensitive personal data.

**Technical Data:** IP addresses, cookie data, and browsing behavior via analytics tools (see Section 7).

---

## 2. How We Collect Your Data

We collect data when you:

- Fill out a contact or inquiry form on our website (anonymous inquiries accepted)
- Book a session through our booking system
- Make payments through third-party payment processors
- Visit our website, where cookies automatically collect technical data

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

- Schedule and manage your boudoir session
- Send confirmations, preparation guides, and follow-up communications
- Process payments and deliver your final images and products (prints, albums, digital files)
- Send appointment reminders and post-session information
- Send marketing emails and promotions only with your explicit consent — you may opt out at any time

---

## 4. Your Images — Boudoir-Specific Policy

This section applies specifically to photographs taken during your session and supersedes general photography industry practices.

**Ownership:** Your images are yours. We retain a license to store them for delivery purposes only.

**Portfolio & Social Media Use:** We will **never** use your boudoir images in our portfolio, on social media, on this website, or in any marketing material without your **separate, explicit written consent**. This consent is entirely optional and has no bearing on the quality of your session or products.

**Model Release:** A model release form is optional and separate from your session contract. You may consent to portfolio use, restrict use to specific platforms, require anonymity (cropped face, no identifying details), or decline entirely. You may revoke consent at any time in writing.

**Delivery & Storage:** Your final images are delivered via a secure, password-protected private gallery. Gallery access links are sent only to the email address you provide. Gallery links are not shared with third parties.

**Retention:** Your images are stored securely for 6 months after delivery to allow for reprints and additional orders. At the 5-month mark, you will receive an email reminder that your gallery will be permanently deleted in 30 days, giving you the opportunity to place any final orders. After 6 months, all images are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems. You may request earlier deletion at any time by contacting us.

**Third-Party Labs:** If you order print products (albums, wall art, prints), your images are transmitted securely to our print lab for production only. Print labs are contractually prohibited from retaining or using your images for any other purpose.

**No Unauthorized Access:** Your images are never accessible to anyone other than you and Noran Shine Studio staff directly involved in your session and order fulfillment.

---

## 5. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only with:

- **Payment processors** (e.g., Stripe) — for secure transaction processing
- **Email platforms** — for session communications and (with your consent) marketing
- **Analytics tools** — anonymized technical data only (see Section 7)
- **Print labs** — only for fulfillment of your product orders, under strict confidentiality

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

- SSL encryption on all web pages and forms
- Password-protected private galleries for image delivery
- Secure, encrypted storage for all session files
- Limited staff access to client data — only those directly serving your session
- Secure deletion of images upon request or after the retention period

---

## 7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to improve your experience and understand how our website is used:

- **Analytics** (e.g., Google Analytics) — to analyze site traffic anonymously
- **Marketing pixels** — only with your consent, for relevant advertising

You may manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use the website or book a session.

---

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Under Florida and U.S. privacy laws, you have the right to:

- **Access your data** — request what information we hold about you
- **Request deletion** — ask us to permanently delete your data and images at any time
- **Withdraw consent** — revoke model release consent or marketing consent at any time
- **Opt out of marketing** — unsubscribe from emails at any time
- **Anonymous inquiry** — contact us without providing identifying information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: reply@noran-shine.com
